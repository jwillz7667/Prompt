Promptomize transforms your ideas into powerful, optimised prompts for any AI platform. Whether you're generating text, images, videos, music, code, or 3D models, Promptomize ensures your prompts deliver the best possible results.

HOW IT WORKS
Type what you want to achieve, select your target modality, and tap Enhance. Our advanced AI engine rewrites your prompt using proven prompt engineering techniques — or switch to Chat mode for open-ended conversation.

TWO MODES
Optimise mode refines and strengthens your prompts with expert-level prompt engineering. Chat mode gives you a general-purpose AI assistant for brainstorming, research, and creative exploration.

MAX MODE
Activate MAX Mode for the most advanced enhancement available — deeper reasoning, stronger structure, and PhD-level prompt construction. Available with daily quota based on your plan.

7 CREATIVE MODALITIES
Text, Image, Video, Music, Audio, Code, and 3D. Each modality tailors your prompt for its target output — with platform-aware suggestions and optimised phrasing.

POWER TOOLS
After enhancing, unlock three premium tools:
- Variations: Generate alternative versions of your prompt to find the perfect wording
- Sandbox: Test your prompt across multiple AI platforms side-by-side
- Workflows: Automate multi-step prompt sequences for complex projects

PLATFORM OPTIMISATION
Fine-tune prompts for specific AI platforms. Each platform has unique strengths and constraints — Promptomize adapts your prompt automatically for the best results.

PROJECT CONTEXTS
Attach reusable project contexts to keep your tone, terminology, and goals consistent across sessions — no need to repeat background information.

CONVERSATION THREADS
Refine prompts through multi-turn conversations. Each turn builds on the previous result for increasingly precise outputs.
